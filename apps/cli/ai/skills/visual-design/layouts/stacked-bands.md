title: "Stacked bands"
description: "The classic page: a full-bleed cover hero, then one full-width band per section stacked down the page, each band a different background from the one before it, each holding its content on the wide grid — text beside image for the story, visit, and contact sections, a row of cards for the menu, products, or services, with the section title on the left and a short note or link on the right above the cards. The header floats over the hero and the footer is the last band."
---
Build: hero as a cover block (`alignfull`, `min-height` around 80–100dvh) with the headline and call to action pinned center-left over a scrimmed image, never centered; every following section an `alignfull` group with its own palette background and block padding of roughly 6–8rem, alternating light and dark or two light tones so consecutive bands never share a background; inside each band an `alignwide` layout — a vertically centered two-column split (text beside image) for story, visit, and contact, a three-column card row for the menu, products, or services, preceded by a heading row with the title left and a note or link right; `settings.layout.contentSize` 760px and `wideSize` 1240px in `theme.json` so bands run edge to edge while their contents stay on the wide grid; the header floats over the hero with a transparent background, so its text and logo must contrast with the hero image and its scrim; the footer is the last band.
Fallback: columns stack in source order below 782px; the hero keeps its `min-height` but drops to 70dvh on short screens.
