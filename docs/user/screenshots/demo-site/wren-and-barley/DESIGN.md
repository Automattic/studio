# Wren & Barley

## Overview

A neighborhood bakery and café that feels like the shop itself: warm terracotta and cream, one serif doing all the talking, and photos of bread doing the rest. Quiet, unhurried, a little old-fashioned.

## Colors

Terracotta (`primary`) is the brand: the header band, buttons and links. Pages sit on white (`background`) with dark umber-brown text (`text`). Cream and sand (`accent-2`, `accent-5`) fill alternating sections; taupe (`accent-3`) is for dates and quiet captions, umber (`accent-4`) for secondary text on cream.

## Typography

Literata throughout. Headings, the site title, the navigation and buttons are uppercase with wide letter spacing; body text is regular weight at a comfortable reading size.

## Layout

Full-width bands that alternate white and cream, each holding one idea. Text columns stay narrow; photos run wide.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat. No shadows; sections are separated by color, not depth.

## Shapes

Square corners everywhere, buttons included. A thin terracotta rule marks quotes.

## Components

The primary button is solid terracotta with white uppercase label text, and turns umber-brown on hover.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do let the photos carry the warmth. Don't add rounded corners, gradients or a second typeface.

## Imagery

Natural-light photos of bread, pastries and the shop, shown untreated.

## Motion

None beyond the browser's own hover transitions.

## Voice

Friendly and plain, like the person behind the counter: "Fresh from the oven", not "artisanal baked goods experience".
