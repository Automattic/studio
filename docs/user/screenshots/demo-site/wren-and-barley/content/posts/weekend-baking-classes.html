Starting in November we are opening the bakery on Saturday mornings for small classes. Six people, three hours, one loaf each to take home along with a jar of our starter.

You will mix, fold, shape and score a country loaf under Ana's eye, and we will talk through what to do when things go wrong at home, because they will. All tools and flour are provided. Bring an apron and some patience.

Classes run from 9am to noon and cost 85 per person, which includes breakfast from the counter. Email us to book a place.
