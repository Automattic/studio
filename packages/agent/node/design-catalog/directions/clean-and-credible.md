---
title: "Clean and credible"
description: "The site a trustworthy firm would have: a pure white ground, navy for headlines and the primary button, cool gray surfaces for cards and alternate sections, a neutral sans at moderate weights, small radii, and tidy rows of evidence — credentials, numbers, testimonials. Nothing clever; everything in order. For clinics, law and accounting firms, consultancies, and software sold to businesses."
---
Palette: white `#ffffff`, navy `#1b2a4a` for headlines, the primary button, and one full navy section, cool gray surfaces `#f3f5f8` and `#e4e8ee` for cards and alternate sections, text `#1f2733`, secondary text `#5b6675`, one accent (teal `#0f8a8a` or cobalt `#2457d6`) for links and icons only.
Type: one neutral sans (Public Sans, Source Sans 3, IBM Plex Sans, or Figtree) at 600 for headlines at `clamp(2rem, 4.5vw, 3.5rem)` with `line-height: 1.15` and `letter-spacing: -0.01em`; body 1.0625rem/1.6; labels uppercase at 0.75rem with `letter-spacing: 0.08em`; tabular numerals (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) for stats.
Surface: flat white; cards on the gray surface with a 1px border in `#e4e8ee` and a single soft shadow (`0 1px 3px rgb(16 24 40 / 0.06)`); one navy section with white text carrying the numbers or the call to action.
Shapes: `border-radius: 6px` on cards, buttons, inputs, and images; hairline rules between rows; a 3px accent bar along the top of a featured card; checkmarks and simple line icons in the accent.
Imagery: photography of people and places in natural light with a neutral grade (`filter: saturate(0.95)`), or clean product screenshots on the gray surface with a 1px border; client or accreditation logos in a monochrome row.
Motion: 150–200ms ease-out on hover; cards lift 2px; nothing on scroll.
Avoid: warm tints, cream, gradients, decorative shapes, poster-scale display type, more than one accent, playful or rounded faces.
