---
title: "Scandinavian"
description: "Cool, light, and quiet: a near-white ground with a hint of blue-gray, pale birch and slate as the only tones, a humanist sans at moderate weight, generous whitespace, thin rules, and no ornament. The cool sibling of Soft neutral — calm from restraint rather than warmth. For furniture, architecture, product studios, homeware, and services that want to feel clear."
---
Palette: ground `#f7f8f9`, white `#ffffff` for cards and alternate sections, birch `#e9e4dc` as the one warm tone for a single section or the footer, slate `#3b4552` for headlines, text `#2a3038`, secondary `#6f7884`, rules `#dde1e6`, one accent (deep blue `#2b4c7e` or forest `#2f5d4a`) for links and the primary button.
Type: a humanist sans (Manrope, Hanken Grotesk, or Albert Sans) at 500 for headlines at `clamp(2rem, 5vw, 4rem)` with `letter-spacing: -0.02em` and `line-height: 1.1`; body 1.0625rem/1.65; labels in sentence case in the secondary color, no tracking.
Surface: flat; white cards on the ground with a 1px rule and no shadow; sections separated by whitespace (6–10rem) and a single 1px rule, never by a color change except the one birch section.
Shapes: `border-radius: 4px` at most; hairlines; images in 4/3 or 1/1 frames; wide margins; nothing decorative.
Imagery: photography in cool daylight with a neutral grade (`filter: saturate(0.9) brightness(1.03)`) of objects, rooms, and materials; no illustration.
Motion: 250ms ease-out on hover; images fade in over 400ms; nothing else.
Avoid: cream or beige grounds, warm tints beyond the one birch section, saturated color, shadows, pill buttons, decorative shapes, display serifs, heavy weights.
