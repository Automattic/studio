---
title: "Four-tile cover"
description: "The viewport is divided into four equal squares that together form the cover: one holds the headline, one an image, one a solid field with a single line, one the call to action. The tiles hold the first screen as a fixed composition until the visitor scrolls past them, and the same 2×2 grid reappears as a motif for section openers."
---
Build: the header is a slim in-flow bar (`position: sticky; top: 0`) with an opaque background, sitting above the tiles in the page flow; below it the cover is a 2×2 group with the grid layout in its markup (`"layout":{"type":"grid","columnCount":2}`) sized to the remaining viewport (`height: calc(100dvh - <header height>); grid-template: 1fr 1fr / 1fr 1fr`) so all four tiles are fully visible at load, each tile a group or cover block; every tile carries its own opaque background (a palette color, a gradient, or an image) — a tile that inherits the page background reads as empty space and the cover bleeds into the header; `gap: 0` on the grid, or a stripe of page background opens between the rows; section openers reuse a half-height 2×2 pattern in every other section at most, or the page turns into a checkerboard; image tiles may respond to hover with a slow scale of the image inside its clipped cell (`overflow: hidden` on the cell, `transform: scale(1.04)` on the image over 600ms), while the cell itself keeps its size.
Fallback: tiles stack into a single column on small screens.
