title: "Aurora"
description: "Modern and light: a white ground with one large soft mesh gradient — two or three hues blurred together — glowing behind the hero and once more near the end, a contemporary grotesk in tight lowercase, thin gray rules, pill buttons, and product imagery floating on the glow. The light counterpart of Liquid glass, without the glass. For apps, startups, agencies, and anything that wants to feel new."
---
Palette: white `#ffffff`, ink `#0f1222` for text, gray `#6b7080` for secondary text and `#e8eaf0` for rules; two or three gradient hues at full saturation — sky `#4f8cff`, coral `#ff6f61`, and violet `#8b5cf6`, or a two-hue pair — used only inside the gradient and on the primary button, never as a flat fill.
Type: a contemporary grotesk (Instrument Sans, Onest, or Plus Jakarta Sans) at 600 for headlines at `clamp(2.5rem, 7vw, 6rem)` with `letter-spacing: -0.04em` and `line-height: 1`, lowercase; body at 1.0625rem/1.6; labels inside a pill with a 1px gray border.
Surface: flat white; the gradient is two or three absolutely positioned `border-radius: 50%` divs in the hues with `filter: blur(80px)` at 60–70% opacity, clipped by the section, behind the hero and one lower section only — the rest of the page is white with hairlines; cards carry a 1px `#e8eaf0` border and no shadow; one soft colored shadow under the primary button (`0 8px 24px rgb(79 140 255 / 0.3)`).
Shapes: `border-radius: 12–16px` on cards and images, `999px` on buttons and labels; a 1px rule under the header; screenshots and renders may be cropped at the bottom edge of their section.
Imagery: product screenshots or renders floating on the glow with a thin border; photography, if any, in a rounded frame with a neutral grade; thin monoline icons in ink.
Motion: the gradient hues drift slowly (a 20–30s translate loop, off under reduced motion); 400ms fades with a 10px rise on scroll; hover darkens the primary button slightly.
Avoid: purple as a flat fill, the gradient on every section, glass or blur on elements, cream, serifs, hard shadows, Inter as the display face, dark sections.
