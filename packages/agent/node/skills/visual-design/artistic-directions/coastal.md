Fresh rather than sweet, calm rather than corporate. For travel, spas, marinas, seaside restaurants and rentals, swimwear, and airlines."

Palette: white `#ffffff`, sea blue `#2a7fb8` for one or two full section fields and the primary button, powder `#dbeaf5` as a light field for cards and alternate sections, sand `#f1e9dc` for a single warm section, navy `#12314f` for headlines and text, one small accent (coral `#ef7d5c`) for a highlight or a tag; the page is mostly white and powder, with sea blue as the strong note.
Type: a clean humanist sans (Nunito Sans, Figtree, or Hanken Grotesk) at 600 for headlines at `clamp(2.25rem, 5.5vw, 4.5rem)` with `letter-spacing: -0.02em` and `line-height: 1.1`; body at 1.0625rem/1.65; labels uppercase at 0.75rem with `letter-spacing: 0.1em` in sea blue.
Surface: flat fields; white cards on powder with no border and one soft cool shadow (`0 4px 16px rgb(18 49 79 / 0.08)`); no gradients except a light scrim on photographs.
Shapes: `border-radius: 12px` on cards, images, and inputs, `999px` on buttons and tags; a gentle wave edge between sections from an inline SVG, used once or twice; thin monoline icons in sea blue.
Imagery: sunlit photography of water, sky, sand, and coastal light with a bright airy grade (`filter: brightness(1.04) saturate(1.05)`), cropped into rounded rectangles; never dark or moody.
Motion: 300ms ease-out on hover with a 2px lift on cards; images fade in over 400ms; a slow float on the wave edge; nothing bouncy.
Avoid: cream grounds, black, dark sections, neon, heavy display type, serifs, sharp corners, more than one warm section, pastel candy colors.
