---
title: "Lookbook"
description: "The photographs are the design: a white ground, full-color photography left exactly as shot, one sans at two weights set small and quiet, and almost no chrome — no cards, no icons, no accent color, no decoration. Whatever the pictures bring is the palette. For restaurants, architects, hotels, photographers, makers, and any business whose work is best seen."
---
Palette: white `#ffffff`, near-black `#151515` for text, mid gray `#767676` for captions; no accent color — the call to action is black on white or white on a photograph; a brand color from the brief appears once, on the primary button.
Type: one sans (Figtree, DM Sans, or Inter Tight) at two weights: 500 for headlines at `clamp(1.75rem, 3.5vw, 2.75rem)` with `letter-spacing: -0.01em`, deliberately modest so the images stay the loudest thing on the page, and 400 for body at 1rem/1.6; labels uppercase at 0.6875rem with `letter-spacing: 0.12em`.
Surface: flat white; no cards, no borders, no shadows; text over a photograph sits on a bottom gradient scrim (`linear-gradient(transparent, rgb(0 0 0 / 0.55))`).
Shapes: square corners everywhere; images in 3/2 or 4/5 frames with 8–16px gutters; the only rules are 1px hairlines in the header and footer.
Imagery: full color, large and frequent, with a light natural grade (`filter: contrast(1.03)`) and never desaturated or tinted — every image is editable and captioned in the label style.
Motion: images fade in over 500ms as they enter; a slow 6s settle from `scale(1.04)` to `scale(1)` on the hero image (off under reduced motion); hover reveals a caption; no other movement.
Avoid: icons, cards, colored fields, decorative shapes, illustration, large display type, filters that change a photograph's color, cream grounds.
