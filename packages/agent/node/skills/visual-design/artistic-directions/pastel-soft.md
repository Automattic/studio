title: "Pastel soft"
description: "Light and airy: a white ground, three pastels at low saturation — lavender, mint, blush — as large soft fields, a light geometric sans, big radii, thin line icons, a lot of air, and slow gentle motion. Sweet without being childish; for wellness, beauty, stationery, nurseries, and anything that wants to feel calm and kind."
---
Palette: white `#ffffff`, lavender `#e6e0f8`, mint `#dff3ea`, blush `#fbe4e6`, butter `#fff3d1` as section and card fields at full strength (never tinted further), ink `#3a3550` for text, one deeper accent (plum `#7c5cbf` or sea green `#2f9e83`) for buttons and links so the page still has a focus.
Type: a light geometric sans (Outfit, Urbanist, or Poppins at 300–500) for headlines at `clamp(2.25rem, 6vw, 5rem)`, weight 400, `line-height: 1.1`; body in the same face or Nunito Sans at 1.0625rem/1.65; labels lowercase in the accent.
Surface: flat pastel fields; cards in a pastel on white or white on a pastel, no borders; one shadow in a tint of the field (`0 12px 32px rgb(124 92 191 / 0.12)`) on the hero card only; no gradients.
Shapes: `border-radius: 24–32px` on cards and images, `999px` on buttons and tags; large soft circles in a pastel behind the hero image; wavy section edges from an inline SVG; nothing sharp.
Imagery: bright high-key photography with lifted shadows (`filter: brightness(1.05) contrast(0.95)`), cropped into rounded rectangles or circles; thin monoline icons in the accent.
Motion: 400–600ms fades with an 8px rise on scroll; buttons brighten on hover; a slow float on the background circles; nothing bouncy.
Avoid: saturated candy colors, black, cream or beige grounds, heavy type, black borders, gray drop shadows, more than four pastels.
