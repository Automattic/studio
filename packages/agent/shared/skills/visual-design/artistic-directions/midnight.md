Premium and calm after dark, without Art Deco's gilt or Noir's austerity. For finance, hospitality, travel, nautical brands, and anything that wants to feel composed and expensive."
---
Palette: navy `#0f1b33` as the ground, a slightly lighter blue `#172743` for panels and alternate sections, a muted blue `#3a4f78` for rules and secondary text, cream `#f3ede0` for text, one warm accent — gold `#d4a853`, coral `#f0876a`, or amber `#e8a33d` — for the primary button, a headline word, and links; no black and no pure white.
Type: a serif with some elegance (Cormorant Garamond at 500, Playfair Display at 400, or Fraunces) for headlines at `clamp(2.5rem, 6.5vw, 5.5rem)` with `line-height: 1.05`, or a humanist sans (Albert Sans or Figtree at 500) for a more modern feel; body in a humanist sans at 1.0625rem/1.6 in cream at 85% opacity; labels uppercase at 0.75rem with `letter-spacing: 0.14em` in the muted blue.
Surface: flat navy; panels in the lighter blue with a 1px border in the muted blue at 40% opacity; no drop shadows; an optional slow radial glow of the lighter blue behind the hero (`radial-gradient(ellipse at top, #1d3159, transparent 60%)`).
Shapes: square corners or a 4px radius at most; hairlines in the muted blue; a single 2px accent rule under the hero headline; wide margins and generous vertical space.
Imagery: photography with cool shadows and warm highlights (`filter: contrast(1.05) saturate(0.9)`) under a navy gradient scrim where it meets text; night, water, interiors, and skylines suit it; no borders on images.
Motion: 600ms fades with a 12px rise on scroll; the accent underline draws in on link hover; nothing bouncy or fast.
Avoid: black, pure white, cream grounds, a second accent, saturated blues, rounded pills, heavy grotesk headlines, neon, glass or blur effects.
