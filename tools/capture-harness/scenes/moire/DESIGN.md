# Moire

## Overview

A slow-fashion journal that dresses like its subject: one loud color, one dark one, and the cloth in the photos doing the rest. Confident, tactile and a little playful.

## Colors

Dia yellow (`base`) is the page; deep brown (`contrast`, `primary`) is the type, the wordmark and the buttons. Pale and warm yellows (`secondary`, `tertiary`) fill panels and highlights.

## Typography

Poppins carries the voice: a huge black-weight wordmark, headings in heavy capitals, small uppercase labels for navigation and dates. Libertinus Serif sets the reading text of paragraphs, lists and quotes.

## Layout

One wide column with photos edge to edge of it; the wordmark fills the width of the page.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat. Color and scale make the hierarchy, not shadows.

## Shapes

Barely rounded: buttons have a quarter-rem radius, photos are square.

## Components

The primary button is deep brown with light text in uppercase Poppins.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do let the yellow and the photos carry the page. Don't add a third typeface or soften the brown to grey.
