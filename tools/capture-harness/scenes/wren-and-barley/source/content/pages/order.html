Order by 4pm for pickup the next morning, and we will have it boxed and waiting.

How to order

  1. Pick anything from the menu, or a box of six or twelve pastries.
  2. Call (555) 014-2014 or email orders@wrenandbarley.example with your name and a pickup time.
  3. We confirm by the end of the day. Pay at the counter when you collect.

Good to know

  • Whole loaves can be sliced on request.
  • Celebration cakes need three days' notice.
  • Orders over 40 get a free bag of our house granola.
