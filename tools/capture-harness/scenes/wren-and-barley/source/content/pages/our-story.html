Wren & Barley started with one oven, a folding table and a Saturday market pitch.

In 2014 we baked forty loaves a week in a rented kitchen and sold them before ten. The starter from those first loaves is still the one we feed every evening. It has moved house twice, survived a heatwave and a broken fridge, and it has never missed a bake.

The shop on Mill Lane opened in 2018. We kept the oven in view because people like to see the bread come out, and because it keeps us honest. If a batch is not right, you will see it too.

Good bread is mostly patience. We just make sure nothing gets in its way.

Ana, head baker

Today there are six of us. We mill a share of our flour from grain grown an hour away, buy fruit from the market we started at, and still close on Mondays to rest the oven and ourselves.
