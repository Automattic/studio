The market has turned to apples and pumpkins, which means the counter has too. From this week you will find apple galettes, pumpkin scones and a pear and hazelnut tart that we sell by the slice.

The galettes use a mix of Bramley and Cox apples from the Hilltop orchard, so they hold their shape and still taste of something. The pastry is the same rough puff we use for the morning buns.

Spiced lattes are back on the drinks board as well. The autumn menu runs until the first week of December, or until the orchard runs out, whichever comes first.
